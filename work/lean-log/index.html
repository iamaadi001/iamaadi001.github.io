The idea

A vlog series about discipline and getting 1% better every day, told through my own training. Every talk mixes my experience, fitness and one lesson about growth.

How it runs

I film. Reels, captions and thumbnails are built with AI tools. Two reels go out per platform every day, and a long vlog for each training day.

Run by the numbers

Every Sunday I review the analytics and change hooks, titles and posting times from what the numbers say.

First four weeks

8,536 views on YouTube in 28 days and 7,982 on Instagram, with 85.6% of the Instagram views coming from people who did not follow me yet.
