Content, planned ahead

The task was to post every day and come up with ideas each morning. Nobody asked for a calendar, so I planned and scheduled 60 days in advance, with posting times, hashtags, audio trends and hook-based reels. The team stopped starting from zero every day.

What it did

The reels reached 30,000+ organic views on Instagram in about two months and 88,651 views on TikTok across about 20 videos. Together that is 118,000+ views.

Selling

I sold and closed 20+ new bookings, with a strategy tailored to each client, and handled the follow-ups for 50+ existing reservations.

A channel from scratch

I built a hotel partnership channel, taking flyers to 80+ hotels to bring in referral bookings.

Automation with the CEO

I worked directly with the CEO to automate 3 recurring workflows with AI tools, which cut his weekly admin time by 20%. I used n8n, Claude Code and Apify every day.

A promo for the shop screen

A muted promo that runs on the screen in the shop, cut from the company's own photos and clips.
